Controls

D-PAD
Fly in 8 directions (drag on the pad). In mission 5 it aims the gun-cam crosshair.
A
Fire. Cadet and Pilot fire on their own. Your shots knock enemy bullets out of the air. In mission 6 (no guns): dash when the BOOST meter is full.
B
Kryptid Bomb: clears bullets, hurts everything. In mission 6: shield pulse.
START
Pause (then SELECT quits the mission). On the mission map: Forge's Armory.
SELECT
Hold it and press A on the mission map to practice that boss. On the title, hold it and press B to erase saves.

The bounties

  1. Rimeveil Lane: krakenlings, falling ice, Nubs' scrap gunship, then the Rimemaw (old Earth: kraken).
  2. Stormwall Nebula: storm wind, eels, thunderclouds, then the Voltwake (old Earth: thunderbird).
  3. Barrens Belt: imps, wisps, a stomp to dodge, Nubs again, then the Ashmare (old Earth: Jersey Devil).
  4. Frostbite Moon: mine turrets, frost bats, the Brayhound, then Marrow (old Earth: wendigo).
  5. Lamp Drift: the helm is jammed, so you work the gun cam: aim the crosshair, shoot down saucers, rocks and green orbs, then Lamptall.
  6. Escape Run: a rival gang knocks your guns out. No shooting: dodge, graze and dash your way down to the planet.
  7. Score high and the boss is captured (triple pay); a good run kills it (double); a weak one lets it escape. Spend your pay at Forge's Armory.
  8. All six missions are open from the start: play them in any order.
  9. Arcade: move past the last mission on the star chart. Pick any of the seven ships and fly missions 1-4 on a loop until your ships run out. Each map runs twice with no break: warp at the end of lap 1, then lap 2 in new colours with angrier enemies and the boss. Grab capsules for the arcade-only weapons: Prism, Helix and Nova.
