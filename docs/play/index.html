Controls

D-PAD
Fly in 8 directions (drag on the pad). In mission 5 it aims the gun-cam crosshair.
A
Fire. Cadet and Pilot fire on their own. Your shots knock enemy bullets out of the air. In mission 6 (no guns): dash when the BOOST meter is full.
B
Kryptid Bomb: clears bullets, hurts everything. In mission 6: shield pulse.
START
Pause. On the mission map: Forge's Armory
SELECT
Hold it and press A on the mission map to practice that boss. On the title, hold it and press B to erase saves.

The bounties

  1. Rimeveil Lane: krakenlings, the ice ring, Nubs' scrap gunship, then the Space Kraken.
  2. Stormwall Nebula: Impundulu lightning birds, Lindworms, thunderclouds, Mothman, then the Thunderbird.
  3. Barrens Belt: Pukwudgies, Devil's eggs, will-o'-the-wisps, Nubs Mk II, then the Jersey Devil.
  4. Frostbite Moon: mine turrets, frost bats, Tatzelwurms, ice storms, the Beast of Bray Road, then the Wendigo.
  5. Flatwoods Drift: the helm is jammed, so you work the gun cam: aim the crosshair, shoot down saucers, rocks and green orbs, then the Flatwoods Monster.
  6. Score high and the boss is captured (triple pay); a good run kills it (double); a weak one lets it escape. Spend your pay at Forge's Armory.
  7. Fly into a sleeping baby cryptid to make it your wingman.
